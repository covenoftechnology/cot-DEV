APEX · CONJURATION · MECHANIC

The Apex Incantation: Triggers Without Tears

O
Inscribed by the Oracle of Orgs
12 min read · 4 incantations within
[ illustration · 1280×640 ]

Every trigger you write is a binding spell — speak it carelessly and it fires a thousand times in the dark, recursing until your governor limits collapse. Speak it with intent, and a single incantation governs every record that crosses your altar.

One trigger per object

The first law of the craft: bind one trigger to each object and let a handler class carry the logic. Scatter your spells across many triggers and you forfeit all control over the order in which they wake.

"A trigger should decide nothing and delegate everything. It is the doorway, not the room."

// the incantation
trigger AccountTrigger on Account (before insert) {
AccountHandler.conjure(Trigger.new);
}

Master this rite and the next scrolls — bulkification, recursion wards, and the trigger framework — will open to you.

Mystic Terms & Privacy

The pact between you and the coven — what we ask of you, what we gather, and how we guard it.

LAST INSCRIBED · THE NEW MOON, JUNE 2026

Welcome, seeker. By entering the Coven of Technology you accept the terms below and entrust us with the care of your data. We keep this plain and honest — no cursed fine print.

✦ The Pact — using the coven

The Coven of Technology is a place of learning. You may read the scrolls, attempt the trials, and earn your rank for your own growth and craft. The scrolls, trials, and teachings remain the intellectual property of the coven and its authors — you're welcome to learn from them, but not to resell or redistribute them without written consent. Use the coven kindly: no harassment of fellow members, no abuse of the platform, no dark magic.

⟡ What we gather

To welcome you into the circle we collect what you give us — your coven name and email when you register. As you study, we record your learning progress: scrolls read, trial scores, Aether earned, and your rank. We also note basic technical details such as browser and device type, and anonymized usage patterns that help us improve the craft.

✷ How the Aether flows

We use your data to track your progress, power the ranks and the coven leaderboard, recommend the next scroll, and keep your account secure. We may send you the occasional missive about new scrolls or coven happenings — you can silence these at any time. We never sell your personal data to anyone, in this realm or another.

☽ Cookies & scrying

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your progress, plus optional analytics cookies that show us how the coven is used. You may clear or decline the optional ones at any time through your browser without losing your place.

✶ Third-party spirits

A few trusted familiars help us run the coven — hosting, analytics, and email providers who process data on our behalf under their own safeguards. We share only what they need to serve you, and never more.

⚖ Your rights as a member

The coven serves you, not the other way around. You may request access to your data, correct it, export it, or ask us to erase it entirely. You can withdraw consent or unsubscribe whenever you wish. Send a raven (or an email) and we'll honor your request promptly.

⚿ Guarding & keeping

We keep your data only while your account lives, and protect it with reasonable wards and safeguards. No spell is perfectly unbreakable, but we treat your trust as sacred and act quickly should anything go amiss.
